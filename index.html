About — local models & DevTools

I build with local AI models — Qwen, DeepSeek and small quantised models running on ordinary hardware — and I write about what they do when nobody is watching. Most of my work starts from a failure I hit myself: a tool call that returns an empty result when it should have raised, a model that reports success without performing the action, a workflow that quietly costs more than the hosted alternative it replaced.

My background is in fire alarm and security systems: ten years building to code, seven documenting life-safety failures. That work teaches a specific habit — a system that reports “all clear” is not the same as a system that is working, and the difference is only ever settled by a test. I apply that to AI tooling.

The result is writing aimed at the developer who has to run it: complete code, real terminal output, honest failure modes, and no step skipped because it looked obvious to the author.
